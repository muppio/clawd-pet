# Code of conduct

Clawd Pet is a small, friendly project. Let's keep it that way.

## How to behave

- Be kind and patient. Everyone here gives their time for free.
- Assume good faith, and help newcomers find their way.
- Disagree about ideas and code, not about people.
- No harassment of any kind. That includes insults, slurs, personal attacks, trolling, unwanted sexual attention, threats, and posting someone's private details.

This applies in issues, pull requests, reviews, comments, and anywhere else you speak for the project.

## What maintainers can do

Maintainers can edit, hide or delete comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people from the repository when these rules are broken. A friendly warning usually comes first, but not always.

## Reporting a problem

If someone makes you feel unwelcome or unsafe, contact the maintainer, muppio, privately through GitHub, for example with the contact details on muppio's GitHub profile. Please don't raise it in a public issue. Reports are kept private.

You can also use GitHub's own **Report content** option on any comment. That goes to GitHub's staff.
