Codex got a pet. Claude Code deserved one too.

Clawd Pet puts a little pixel Clawd in the corner of your screen. He talks, he listens, he watches Claude work, and now and then he spars with your Codex pet.

Eight moods

He shows you what's going on without a word

Every pose is drawn on the same 16 by 13 pixel grid and changes with what Claude is doing.

  • IdleBlinks, breathes and looks around.
  • ListeningArms up and wide-eyed while you talk.
  • WorkingWalks in place under a spinner while Claude works.
  • Needs youRaises a "!" when Claude is waiting for your OK.
  • TalkingHis mouth moves with each word of a reply.
  • DoneHappy eyes, rosy cheeks and a little hop.
  • NappingDozes off after five quiet minutes.
  • Lost a sparDizzy, with stars circling his head.

Type, dictate or talk

A pet you can actually talk to

Clawd stands on a composer like the one under the Codex pet. Type a question and press Return, tap the mic to dictate, or tap the orb for a hands-free voice conversation.

  1. You talkTap the orb and ask anything.
  2. Your Mac writes it downOn the device, whenever macOS supports that for your language.
  3. Your own claude -p answersNo API key. Replies count toward your Claude plan.
  4. Clawd says it out loudSentence by sentence, as the reply streams in.

Live from Claude Code

He keeps an eye on Claude while you look away

The Claude Code mod tells Clawd what each turn is doing: which file Claude is reading, which command is running and which project it's in. When Claude stops to ask for your OK, he raises a "!". When the work is done, he hops.

Click him or the arrow in his bubble to jump straight to the Claude app.

The main event

Clawd vs. your Codex pet

Right-click Clawd and choose Spar with Codex, or type /pet fight. He picks a fight, runs over, and they vanish into a cartoon dust cloud. Then he walks home with the result.

Clawd 4 · Codex 1 · Draws 1

He finds Codex on his ownClawd runs to the spot Codex saved for its pet, and nothing records your screen. If it isn't out, he asks you to open it first.
It's all in good funClawd wins about 45% of rounds, Codex 40%, and the rest are draws. The score is kept.
Or let them spar on their ownTurn on "Spar now and then" for a rematch every 20 to 40 quiet minutes.

Commands

Three commands and four links

Type thisWhat happens
/petSummon or dismiss Clawd. The first run installs the app in a few seconds.
/pet fightSend Clawd to spar with the Codex pet, which has to be on screen.
/pet rebuildBuild the app yourself from source. This one needs Xcode's command line tools.
clawdpet://ask?q=…Ask Claude something. clawdpet://voice, clawdpet://new and clawdpet://fight work too.

Install

One line in Claude Code

  • macOS 14 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel
  • Or Windows 10 or 11
  • Claude Code installed and logged in

Then type /pet. The first run installs the app in a few seconds, and later plugin updates install themselves. The first time you use the mic or the orb, macOS asks for microphone and speech recognition access.

/plugin install clawd-pet --marketplace muppio/clawd-pet

Privacy

What Clawd hears and sends

The mic is on only when you askIt turns on when you tap the mic or the orb, and stops when you stop talking or tap again.
Speech turns into text on your MacWhenever macOS supports on-device recognition for your language. When it doesn't, macOS uses Apple's speech service.
Only your words go to ClaudeJust what you type or say, sent through your own claude command. Its only tools are web search and web fetch.
No screen recordingHe finds the Codex pet from the spot Codex saves for it, without capturing anything on screen.
